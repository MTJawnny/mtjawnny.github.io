Teferi's Protection

Instant

Until your next turn, your life total can't change and you gain protection from everything. All permanents you control phase out. (While they're phased out, they're treated as though they don't exist. They phase in before you untap during your untap step.)

How It Works

Teferi's Protection Resolved. What Still Gets Through?

Each row is aimed at you or your board on the turn after you cast it. Prevention stops damage, your frozen life total stops the rest, and your phased-out board cannot be touched. A few things still slip past.

The only damage that reaches you doesn't target you. A targeted spell like a burn spell can't be aimed at you at all while protection is up. Non-targeted damage, like combat, is prevented, unless an effect such as Questing Beast makes it unpreventable. Then your frozen life total means the number never drops, but infect poison counters, lifelink, and commander damage toward 21 still land.

Reading The Result

  • It stacks three layers of defense. You can't be targeted, damage to you is prevented, and your life total can't change. A targeted spell can't be aimed at you, untargeted damage is prevented, and anything that would otherwise change your life, like a drain or a "loses X life" line, simply can't.
  • Your whole board phases out. Phased-out permanents are treated as though they do not exist, so targeted removal, board wipes, and "each player sacrifices" effects find nothing of yours to hit. The board comes back untouched on your next untap.
  • The shield is not total. Damage that says it can't be prevented still registers its side effects, and a "you lose the game" line goes through. Neither protection nor a frozen life total answers a loss condition.

Tips

✓ Hold it for a board wipe or a lethal swing

It is an instant. Cast it in response to a Wrath of God already on the stack, or after attackers are declared with a lethal attack incoming. Your permanents phase out and your life freezes before the wipe or the combat damage resolves, so both miss you entirely.

✓ Blank a Torment of Hailfire or a mass edict

A non-targeted punisher like Torment of Hailfire asks you to lose life, discard, or sacrifice. With your life frozen, an empty hand, and your whole board phased out, you have nothing to lose and nothing to give. The spell resolves and does nothing to you.

✓ Phase a permanent out of a removal spell

In response to a removal spell targeting one of your creatures, cast Teferi's Protection. That creature phases out, the removal loses its only legal target, and it does nothing. You save the permanent and protect the rest of your board at the same time.

Misplays

✗ Unpreventable damage is not fully blanked

A spell that targets you can't be aimed at you while protection is up, but damage that doesn't target you can still reach you if it can't be prevented. Combat damage made unpreventable by an effect like Questing Beast is the main case. Your life total stays frozen, so the number never drops, but infect poison counters, lifelink, and commander damage toward 21 all still register. A commander clock keeps ticking even while you look untouchable.

✗ You still lose to a loss condition

Protection and a frozen life total do not stop a "you lose the game" effect. A Thassa's Oracle trigger, drawing from an empty library, a failed Pact upkeep, or an opponent assembling Mechanized Production all still end the game on schedule. This card buys a turn, not immunity.

✗ You can't pay or gain life while it's up

Your life total can't change, so you can't pay a cost that includes losing life. No cracking a fetch land for one life, no paying Force of Will's life cost, and no life gain at all. Sequence those payments before Teferi's Protection resolves, not after.

Combos

Armageddon / Obliterate / Balance One-Sided Wipe

Float your mana, cast a symmetrical wipe, then hold priority and cast Teferi's Protection. Your board phases out and is treated as not existing, so the wipe finds nothing of yours to destroy. Balance even reads your board as zero and forces opponents to match it. The wipe shreds every other board, and your permanents phase back in on your next untap.

Lethal Vapors Turn-Skip Lock

Lethal Vapors has a {0} ability that destroys it and makes you skip your next turn, and you can activate it as many times as you want by holding priority. Stack thousands of activations, then cast Teferi's Protection in response. You skip thousands of turns, and because the protection lasts until your next turn, it never wears off. Note that any player can activate Lethal Vapors, and opponents keep taking their own turns, so you still need a plan for how they lose.

Fine Print

  • Phasing is not exiling. Your permanents never change zones, so phasing out and back in triggers no "enters" or "leaves the battlefield" abilities. Nothing re-enters, so nothing re-triggers.
  • Attachments and counters come back intact. Auras, Equipment, and counters phase out with the permanent they are on and return still attached, with every counter exactly as it was.
  • Tokens survive now. Under current rules tokens phase out and phase back in like any other permanent. They no longer cease to exist while phased out, which changed in 2017.
  • Non-targeted, non-damage effects still reach you. Protection only stops targeting, damage, and being enchanted or equipped. An effect that says "each player discards a card" still makes you discard from your hand.
  • The shield ends exactly as you phase in. Your permanents phase in at the start of your untap step, before you untap, and the protection ends at the same moment. There is no window where you are untapping and still protected.

Rulings last verified: June 2026

Official Rulings

2017-08-25

If a player has protection from everything, it means three things: 1) All damage that would be dealt to that player is prevented. 2) Auras can't be attached to that player. 3) That player can't be the target of spells or abilities.

2017-08-25

Nothing other than the specified events are prevented or illegal. An effect that doesn't target you could still cause you to discard cards, for example. Creatures can still attack you while you have protection from everything, although combat damage that they would deal to you will be prevented.

2017-08-25

Gaining protection from everything causes a spell or ability on the stack to have an illegal target if it targets you.

2017-08-25

Protection from everything will usually prevent damage if it would be dealt to you, but some damage can't be prevented. In this case, because your life total also can't change, that damage has any other effects that it may have aside from causing you to lose that much life (such as effects from lifelink or infect) and triggers and effects can see that damage was dealt even though your life total didn't change.

2017-08-25

You can't pay a cost that includes the payment of any amount of life other than 0 life.

2017-08-25

If a cost includes causing you to gain life (like the alternative cost of an opponent's Invigorate does), that cost can't be paid.

2017-08-25

Effects that would replace having you gain life with some other event won't be able to be applied because it's impossible for you to gain life. The same is true for effects that would replace having you lose life with some other event.

2017-08-25

A permanent phasing out causes a spell or ability on the stack to have an illegal target if it targets that permanent. As a spell or ability tries to resolve, if all its targets are illegal, that spell or ability doesn't resolve and none of its effects happen, including effects unrelated to the target. If at least one target is still legal, the spell or ability does as much as it can to the remaining legal targets, and its other effects still happen.

2017-08-25

If a token is phased out, it will phase in as your next untap step begins. This is a change from previous rules.

2017-08-25

If your untap step is somehow skipped as your next turn begins, your phased-out permanents won't phase in until the next untap step you actually have, but you'll no longer have protection from everything and your life total can change again.

2017-08-25

Any creatures that phase in under your control as your next untap step begins will be able to attack and pay a cost of {T} during that turn.
