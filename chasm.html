Glacial Chasm

Land

Cumulative upkeep—Pay 2 life. (At the beginning of your upkeep, put an age counter on this permanent, then sacrifice it unless you pay its upkeep cost for each age counter on it.)

When this land enters, sacrifice a land.

Creatures you control can't attack.

Prevent all damage that would be dealt to you.

Legacy Vintage Commander Not Modern Not Pioneer Not Standard

How It Works

Glacial Chasm Is Out. What Reaches You?

Each row is aimed at your life total while the Chasm sits in play. Prevention stops damage. It does nothing to direct life loss, and nothing to the upkeep you pay yourself.

Planeswalkers and battles are on their own. The ability reads "dealt to you," so it does not cover damage sent at your planeswalkers or your battles. Those still take combat damage and burn as normal.

Reading The Result

  • Damage is prevented, full stop. Combat damage, burn spells, and damage from abilities are all reduced to nothing while the Chasm is in play. It does not matter how big the number is or where it comes from.
  • Life loss is not damage. An effect that makes you lose life, like a drain spell or a "target player loses X life" line, ignores the Chasm completely. The prevention only ever touches damage.
  • The upkeep is your own life, by choice. The growing payment is a cost you choose to pay, not damage being dealt. The Chasm cannot prevent it, and the cost climbs by two each turn until you stop paying.

Tips

✓ Spend the land's mana before it dies

The sacrifice is a triggered ability, not a cost of playing the land. While that trigger sits on the stack, you still hold priority. Tap the land you plan to give up for mana first, then let the trigger resolve and sacrifice that now-empty land. You keep the mana and lose nothing you were not already losing.

✓ Stifle the enters trigger and keep every land

When Glacial Chasm enters, its "sacrifice a land" trigger goes on the stack. Hold priority and cast Stifle or Trickbind targeting that trigger. It is countered, so no land is sacrificed. You keep the Chasm and all of your other lands, and the damage prevention is already on.

✓ Drop it at instant speed as a fog

Glacial Chasm is a land, so anything that puts a land onto the battlefield at instant speed can deploy it mid-combat. Cast Crop Rotation or activate Elvish Reclaimer after attackers are declared, or with a lethal burn spell on the stack. The Chasm lands first and prevents the damage before it would be dealt.

Misplays

✗ It stops damage, not life loss

The Chasm prevents damage and nothing else. A drain like Exsanguinate, a "loses X life" line like Sign in Blood, and the life you pay for the Chasm's own upkeep all go through untouched. If the source says it deals damage, you are covered. If it says you lose life, you are not.

✗ Unpreventable damage goes straight through

Some damage cannot be prevented at all. Skullcrack, the Stomp half of Bonecrusher Giant, and combat damage from Questing Beast each state that damage can't be prevented, and that overrides the Chasm. The prevention does not apply and the full amount reaches your life total.

✗ Your own creatures cannot attack

The Chasm shuts off your offense, not just your defense. While it is in play none of your creatures can attack, so you cannot race a fast deck or close a game through combat. Plan to remove it, or build around the lock, before you need to swing.

Combos

Solemnity Hard Lock

Solemnity stops counters from being placed. The cumulative upkeep trigger still resolves, but no age counter goes on the Chasm. With zero counters on it, the cost to keep it is two life times zero, which is nothing. The damage prevention becomes permanent and free.

Crucible of Worlds / Ramunap Excavator Combat Window

Both let you play lands from your graveyard. During your upkeep, let the Chasm die instead of paying. Now your creatures can attack, so swing in combat as normal. In your second main phase, replay the Chasm from the graveyard. You get a fresh, zero-counter Chasm and your damage immunity back before you pass the turn.

Life from the Loam + Exploration Engine

A staple of dedicated lands decks. Let the Chasm die to its upkeep, dredge it back to hand with Life from the Loam, then replay it as a second land for the turn off Exploration. The protective shell sustains itself for as long as the engine keeps running.

Thespian's Stage Reset

Activate Thespian's Stage to copy a Glacial Chasm already on the battlefield. The Stage becomes a copy without leaving or re-entering, so the "sacrifice a land" trigger never fires and you skip the land tax. Let the original, with its stacked-up age counters, die to its upkeep while the fresh copy carries the prevention.

Urborg / Yavimaya Mana Fix

The Chasm's worst trait is that it does not tap for mana. With Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth on the field it is also a Swamp and taps for black. With Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth it is also a Forest and taps for green. The dead draw turns into a working land.

Fine Print

  • Infect and wither carry nothing through. An infect or wither attacker aimed at you deals no damage, so you take no poison counters from it. The prevention only covers you, though, so infect or wither damage to your creatures still lands and still leaves counters.
  • Prevented commander damage does not count. If a commander would deal combat damage to you, the Chasm prevents it. Since the damage is never dealt, none of it counts toward the 21 commander-damage threshold.
  • You can play it as your only land. If the enters trigger resolves and you control no other land to give up, you sacrifice Glacial Chasm itself. The card does not stop you from playing it in the first place.
  • The upkeep is optional, every turn. Each upkeep you put the age counter on first, then choose to pay the full cost or sacrifice. There is no partial payment, and the choice is fresh each turn.
