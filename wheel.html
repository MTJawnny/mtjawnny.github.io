Wheel of Misfortune

Sorcery

Each player secretly chooses a number 0 or greater, then all players reveal those numbers simultaneously and determine the highest and lowest numbers revealed this way. Wheel of Misfortune deals damage equal to the highest number to each player who chose that number. Each player who didn't choose the lowest number discards their hand, then draws seven cards.

Legacy Vintage Commander Not Modern Not Pioneer Not Standard
Oracle Text · From Gatherer

How It Works

Four Players Reveal: 10, 5, 0, 0

Everyone writes a number in secret, then all reveal at once. The board sorts into three outcomes.

Player A
Takes 10 damage Discards hand Draws 7
5Middle
Player B
No damage Discards hand Draws 7
0Lowest
Players C & D
No damage Keeps hand Draws 0

Ties go to the extremes. Tie for the highest number and everyone tied takes the damage. Tie for the lowest and everyone tied keeps their hand and draws nothing. If every player picks the same number, it counts as both highest and lowest at once: everyone takes that much damage, and no one draws.

Reading The Result

  • Damage hits only the highest bid. The card deals damage equal to the highest number, and only to the players who chose that number. A bid of 10 deals 10 to whoever bid 10 and nothing to anyone else.
  • The wheel skips only the lowest bid. Every player who did not choose the lowest number discards their hand and draws seven. The lowest bidder is the one player who keeps their cards, and that player draws nothing.
  • Damage and draw are judged separately. A middle bid pays no damage but still wheels to seven. The top bid both takes the damage and wheels. The bottom bid does neither.

Tips

✓ Prevent the damage, then bid as high as you want

In response to your own Wheel, set up damage prevention with Teferi's Protection, or have a protection effect already active such as the turn off The One Ring. On resolution you write down an enormous number. You are guaranteed to be the highest bid, so you discard and draw seven, and the damage is prevented before it lands.

✓ Redirect the damage with Pariah

Put Pariah on Stuffy Doll or Brash Taunter, or equip Pariah's Shield. Bid a large number and let the damage head your way. Pariah redirects it to the creature, and Stuffy Doll or Brash Taunter then deals that much to a player you choose. You take a fresh seven cards while an opponent eats the full bid.

✓ Cap the draw with Narset or Notion Thief

With Narset, Parter of Veils, an opponent forced to discard and draw seven draws at most one card. With Notion Thief, they draw none and you draw those cards instead. Either way you still wheel your own hand to seven while their refill is shut down.

✓ Punish the refill with draw triggers

With Sheoldred, the Apocalypse, Orcish Bowmasters, or Nekusar, the Mindrazer on the board, every opponent who refills to seven takes extra damage or life loss for the cards they draw. Their only out is bidding the lowest number, which saves them the damage but stalls their hand for the turn.

Misplays

✗ Drawing into lifegain will not save you

If a high bid would put you at zero or less, you lose as the spell finishes resolving. The game checks for that the moment Wheel of Misfortune is done, before you get priority. A Nourishing Shoal or Autochthon Wurm drawn off the wheel is already too late to cast.

✗ You do not pick your number on cast

The secret choice happens on resolution, not when the spell is cast or put on the stack. Counterspells, damage prevention, and any other response have to be set up before it resolves. Once the numbers are revealed, no player gets priority until the entire card has finished.

✗ Everyone bidding the same is not a safe draw

If every player picks the same number, that number is both the highest and the lowest. Every player takes that much damage, and because everyone also chose the lowest number, no one discards or draws. Matching bids is a group damage spell, not a group wheel.

✗ A big Wheel does not kill through commander damage

Even when a commander like Obosh, the Preypiercer is doubling the bid, Wheel of Misfortune is the source of the damage, not the commander. Commander damage only counts combat damage, so 21 from an amplified Wheel does not trigger a commander-damage loss.

Fine Print

  • Choosing in paper. To choose secretly, write your number down and keep it hidden, then reveal all numbers at the same time. A judge can disallow hidden dice or anything that could be changed or read as ambiguous at the reveal.
  • Bids are whole numbers. A chosen number has to be a real, non-negative whole number. You cannot bid infinity, and you cannot bid a fraction.
  • Copies resolve on their own. A copy of Wheel of Misfortune, from Reverberate for example, resolves separately. Players bid, reveal, take damage, and discard or draw for the copy, then do all of it again for the original. Numbers chosen for one do not carry over to the other.

Official Rulings

2020-11-10

For each player to secretly choose a number, that player should write down their chosen number without showing it to anyone else. Each player then keeps their number secret until all players simultaneously reveal their numbers.

2020-11-10

The number of players choosing the highest number doesn't change how much damage is dealt. For example, if the four players in your game reveal 4, 7, 7, and 7, Wheel of Misfortune will deal 7 damage to each of the last three players and those three players will discard their hand and draw seven cards.
