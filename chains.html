Chains of Mephistopheles

Enchantment

If a player would draw a card except the first one they draw in each of their draw steps, that player discards a card instead. If the player discards a card this way, they draw a card. If the player doesn't discard a card this way, they mill a card.

Legacy Vintage Commander Not Modern Not Pioneer Not Standard
Current Oracle Text · From Gatherer

How It Works

One Draw Through The Chains

Run every draw through this in order. A "draw three" is three separate trips, and your hand is rechecked each time.

A draw would happen
Question 1 Is it the first card you would draw this draw step?
Yes Draw it. Your normal turn draw is exempt. Chains does nothing.
No Go to the next question.
Question 2 Do you have at least one card in hand?
Yes Discard 1, then draw 1. Your hand size holds, but you burn through cards.
No Mill 1. Top card of your library to the graveyard. You draw nothing.

It is a replacement, not a trigger. The discard and the mill happen as the draw resolves. Nothing goes on the stack, so once the draw starts no one can respond to it.

Reading The Result

  • Your first draw each turn is free. The normal draw at the start of your draw step is the one exemption. You take it as usual, and everything after it is taxed.
  • Any other draw with cards in hand costs a card. You discard one, then draw one. Your hand size stays flat, but you churn through your deck and feed your own graveyard.
  • Any other draw with an empty hand mills. With nothing to discard, the draw is replaced by putting the top card of your library into your graveyard. You do not draw at all.

Tips

✓ Dredge sidesteps the discard

Dredge and Chains both want to replace the same draw, so you choose the order. Apply dredge first: you return the dredge card and mill its number, and there is no longer a draw left for Chains to touch. You pay no discard. The same trick works with any draw replacement you control, like Abundance.

✓ Madness turns the forced discard into a spell

When Chains makes you discard, pitch a card with madness. Its madness trigger goes on the stack, Chains still hands you your replacement draw, and you cast the discarded card for its madness cost. A penalty meant to cost you a card instead deploys one.

✓ Keep yourself out of the lock

Chains hits everyone, so pair it with a way to skip your own draws. Necropotence skips your draw step and stocks your hand without ever drawing, so nothing of yours gets taxed while the table grinds down. It becomes a one-sided strangle.

Misplays

✗ You cannot respond to it

Chains is a replacement effect, not a triggered ability. The discard and the mill happen as the draw resolves, and nothing is ever put on the stack. There is no window to respond once a draw begins, so do not plan to interrupt the discard or counter it.

✗ Only the first draw of your draw step is free

The exemption covers exactly one card: the normal draw at the start of your draw step. Extra draws that same turn are fully taxed. A Howling Mine draw, a Phyrexian Arena draw, or anything cast on your turn all pay the discard or the mill like any other.

✗ Multi draws are checked one at a time

"Draw three" is three separate draws, not one event. Chains rechecks your hand for each. Cast Brainstorm with an empty hand and you mill three, draw nothing, then put zero cards back because your hand is still empty. One mana spent to deck yourself for three.

Combos

Wheel of Fortune / Timetwister Wheel Lock

A wheel empties every hand first, then tells each player to draw seven. Because every hand is empty when the draws happen, Chains replaces all seven with mills. The whole table dumps its hand and mills seven, and nobody draws a card. You turn a symmetric refill into mass self-mill.

Teferi's Puzzle Box Hand Lock

Each draw step a player takes their free first draw, then Puzzle Box puts their whole hand on the bottom and tells them to draw that many. Their hand is now empty, so every one of those draws mills instead. They reach their main phase with no cards. Their only window is instant speed, after the normal draw and before the Puzzle Box trigger resolves.

Geier Reach Sanitarium / Mikokoro Forced Draw

Activate one of these on an opponent's upkeep, before their draw step. The draw you force on them is not their exempt first draw, so Chains taxes it. They discard a card to draw, or mill if their hand is already empty. Repeat each turn to keep a player stripped down.

Anvil of Bogardan Slow Bleed

Anvil's extra draw happens during the draw step but is not the exempt first draw, so Chains catches it. A player with cards discards then draws for the Anvil draw, then still discards to the Anvil's own clause. The table loses a card a turn while you keep stocking your graveyard.

Fine Print

  • Two copies stack, in order. With two Chains out, each one taxes the same draw in turn. You discard for the first, then discard for the second, then draw. The moment one of them mills you because your hand is empty, that draw is gone and any remaining copies do nothing.
  • Cards drawn as a cost are taxed too. If a spell or ability has you draw as part of its cost, Chains still applies, because a replacement effect can alter how a cost is paid. You discard or mill before that cost is met.
  • It does not loop on its own draw. The draw that Chains hands back after you discard is not taxed again by that same Chains. A replacement effect gets one shot at an event, so there is no infinite discard loop.
  • Your own draw step is fine. Chains is not punishing you for playing it. Your normal first draw each turn is exempt, the same as everyone else's. It only bites on the extra draws.

Official Rulings

2004-10-04

The effect is cumulative. If there are two of these on the battlefield, each of them will modify each draw (after the first one if during the draw step), and will cause the player to discard or to "mill" a card from their library. As they resolve in order, the player must discard if possible. Once the player fails to discard and instead "mills" a card, all further effects of additional Chains of Mephistopheles will not do anything. This is because the "mill" also replaces the draw effect and the player is no longer drawing a card. You handle them in order. Each one makes you discard first and then continue or else mill a card and lose the draw.

2004-10-04

Cards which are drawn as a cost are affected by this card because replacement effects can alter the payment of costs.

2004-10-04

If a spell or ability would cause a player to draw multiple cards, this is treated as a number of individual "draw one card" actions. Apply the effect of Chains of Mephistopheles to each one.

2007-09-16

A player's normal card draw on their turn is exempt from this effect. All other draws will be affected.

2007-09-16

Here's what happens when Chains of Mephistopheles replaces a player's draw: — If that player has at least one card in their hand, they discard a card and then draws a card. — If that player's hand is empty, they put the top card of their library into their graveyard. The player doesn't draw a card at all.
