Tips

✓

Squeeze out one last activation

When the chapter III ability triggers, the Saga isn't sacrificed until that ability finishes resolving. In response to the trigger, tap it for mana and pay to make one more Construct — you lose nothing by getting full value on the way out.

✓

The Constructs keep growing

The tokens check your artifact count constantly, not when they're created. Every artifact you play later — including the one chapter III fetches — makes every Construct bigger immediately.

✓

Know your chapter III hits before you play it

Classic targets: Sol Ring, Skullclamp, Pithing Needle, Shadowspear, or any Mox. Tutoring the right silver bullet at the right time is most of this card's power — have the lines ready.

Common Misplays

✗

"Chapter III finds anything that costs 0 or 1"

It searches for an actual printed mana cost of or — not mana value. A card costing or can't be found, even though its mana value is 1 or 0.

✗

"You can keep it on the battlefield forever"

It's a land, but it's still a Saga. After chapter III resolves, it gets sacrificed — there's no opting out (without effects that remove counters or its abilities).

✗

"When the Saga dies, the Constructs die too"

The tokens are independent permanents. They stay, and they keep their size ability, no matter what happens to the Saga that made them.

Official Rulings

2021-06-18

Urza's Saga is a land, so it can only be played as a land. It cannot be cast as a spell.

2021-06-18

Urza's Saga gains an ability from its first and second chapters. It keeps those abilities for as long as it's on the battlefield.

2021-06-18

While resolving the chapter III ability, you can find only a card with actual mana cost {0} or {1}, not mana value 0 or 1. For example, you couldn't find a card with mana cost {U} or one with mana cost {X}.

2021-06-18

The Construct token has no inherent power or toughness. Its characteristic-defining ability sets its power and toughness equal to the number of artifacts you control, including itself and Urza's Saga.

2021-06-18

The ability that defines the Construct's power and toughness works in all zones, not just the battlefield.

2021-06-18

Even though Urza's Saga is a land, it is also still a Saga, and it will be sacrificed after its third chapter ability resolves.
