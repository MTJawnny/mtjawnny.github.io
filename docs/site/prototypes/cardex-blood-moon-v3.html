Blood Moon

Enchantment
Oracle text

Nonbasic lands are Mountains.

Current official wording · checked October 6, 2026

Old-card warning: The Dark printing says nonbasic lands become basic Mountains. That wording is obsolete. They stay nonbasic.

The short version

Blood Moon turns every nonbasic land on the battlefield into a Mountain. It keeps its name and supertypes, but loses its printed land abilities and other land types. It taps for red.

  • Basic lands: untouched.
  • Fetchlands already in play: cannot fetch. They are just Mountains.
  • Legendary, snow, artifact: those qualities stay unless another rule says otherwise.
  • Your nonbasics too: Blood Moon is symmetrical.

That is the table ruling. If somebody asks “yeah, but why?”, keep scrolling.

OK, but why?

“Mountain” is a basic land type. When an effect sets a land to a basic land type instead of saying it becomes that type in addition to its other types, the rules do more work than the card text shows.

The land loses its old land types and the abilities generated by its rules text, then gains the normal Mountain mana ability. Blood Moon is short because the Comprehensive Rules are carrying the rest of the sentence.

The part people get wrong

It does not make the land basic

“Mountain” is the subtype. “Basic” is a supertype. Blood Moon changes the land type, not the supertype. That is why a Blood Mooned Steam Vents is still a nonbasic land.

The name does not change

Dark Depths is still named Dark Depths. Blood Moon is changing what kind of land it is and what its printed land text does, not renaming the card.

It only affects lands on the battlefield

A Flooded Strand in hand or in a library is still a Flooded Strand. If somebody cracks a fetchland in response to Blood Moon, the ability is already on the stack and resolves normally.

Rules receipts

Comprehensive Rules
  • CR 305.7: setting a land to a basic land type removes old land types and printed abilities and gives the matching mana ability.
  • CR 613: Blood Moon applies as a continuous type-changing effect.
  • CR 614.12: helps determine how Blood Moon affects lands as they enter.
Official rulings
  • Nonbasic lands lose their other land types and abilities and gain the Mountain mana ability.
  • Names and supertypes are not changed. The land does not become basic and is not renamed “Mountain.”
  • Nonbasic lands that normally enter tapped or trigger when they enter can lose those abilities before they apply or trigger.
