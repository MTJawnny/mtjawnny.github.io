How it works

  1. Paste your decklist as plain text. One card per line. Quantities and set codes are fine.
  2. Tag Grabber reads each card's Scryfall function tags, like ramp, removal, and token generation.
  3. The breakdown shows what your deck leans on. The card list finds cards that share your deck's tags.

Tags come from Scryfall's community Tagger project and refresh weekly. Everything runs in your browser. No login, no upload.
